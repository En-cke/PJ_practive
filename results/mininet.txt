=== Lab 9: Mininet dumbbell topology (10 Mbit/s bottleneck, 5ms link delay) ===
Topology: h1,h2 -- s1 == s2 -- h3,h4  (s1-s2 is the bottleneck TCLink)

Run (WSL2 Ubuntu 24.04, OVS 3.3.4, Mininet 2.3.0):
    sudo systemctl start openvswitch-switch
    sudo python3 topology.py --bw 10 --delay 5ms --json results.json
